# Evidence contract — Phase 3A

Interpretation can be probabilistic. Money cannot.

`EvidenceCandidate` is an untrusted typed extraction, not financial authorization. A future extractor targets the JSON format produced by `to_json`; decimal fields are strings and dates have distinct ISO meanings. `from_json` validates transport shape without treating a successful parse as accepted cash. Negative/zero/nonfinite values can reach semantic validation and cannot bypass it by parsing successfully.

`EvidenceSource` carries MESSAGE/IMAGE identity, user/request/event associations, observed timestamp and an optional trusted publisher identity. Message source categories such as bank/employer are not unique publisher identifiers. Unknown image timing/currency stays unknown. Candidate `original_label` preserves the extracted semantic label; it is never interpreted as an instruction.

Fact types distinguish salary, expense/rent, receipt, refund/reimbursement, approved invoice, pending payout, investment value/sale, cancellation, reschedule, employment ended and final payroll. Salary amount versus ongoing change uses scope plus explicit-amendment metadata, not separate enums for every wording. Amount meaning distinguishes net/gross pay, invoice total/balance/paid, tax, subtotal, late fee, valuation, sale proceeds and account balance. Scope distinguishes one event/next occurrence from ongoing/from/until/one-off applicability. Effective, payment, due and period dates are separate; date-dependent bill tiers can be represented as scoped candidates and may remain unresolved if the applicable tier is unknown.

`EvidenceDecision` is ACCEPTED, REJECTED or UNRESOLVED with a machine-readable reason. Only validation can wrap a candidate in `ValidatedEvidenceFact`; later reconciliation can still find conflicts. Confidence is diagnostic and never authorizes cash. `EvidenceBatch.exhaustive` tracks whether a source has been fully accounted for; one accepted number does not establish that a cropped image has been resolved.

Checkpoint A provides schema/transport/tests; B implements validation and deterministic normalized amendments; C connects them through a typed forecast interface; D hardens the boundary after adversarial review. No external extractor, prompt, credential, API or model usage is part of Phase 3A. No sample label belongs in production.

## Deterministic validation and reconciliation (Checkpoint B)

Canonical source records come from trusted request context, not the candidate. User/request/event associations and source timestamps must match. Missing currency/date/amount, nonpositive monetary amounts, unsupported scope, and contradictions stay UNRESOLVED. Clearly hypothetical/conditional income, valuation and contextual gross/subtotal/tax/account-balance figures are REJECTED as non-cash. Confidence has no acceptance threshold. Confirming a pending credit is not settlement; no cash is authorized without the appropriate state.

Reconciliation validates candidates itself and groups by exact linked event or explicit external obligation key. Compatible facts coalesce. Explicit event cancellation takes priority over schedules; a later explicit amendment can supersede older evidence only with the same known publisher identity. Unknown/different publishers and ties remain unresolved. No LLM chooses conflicts. A differing CSV amount requires an explicit amendment; missing outstanding amount requires balance_due semantics. Total is not assumed to equal remaining liability. A fully settled linked bill becomes historical snapshot evidence, not another expense; partial-paid arithmetic remains unresolved without explicit remaining balance.

NormalizedEvidenceState contains immutable event copies, accepted amendment provenance, confirmed future credit identities, and scoped SeriesAmendment directives. It contains no balance or affordability calculation. Next-pay amounts change one event; ongoing amounts explicitly direct later matched projections. Reschedules retain the same event ID; cancelled/paid occurrences get a suppression directive so history cannot re-create them. Final payroll and employment-ended facts stop matched inferred continuation, not unrelated income. New refund/reimbursement/sale/invoice cash is one-off, requires explicit currency/date/category/reference and cannot overwrite a generic matching CSV event. Original linked_event_id values remain lifecycle relationships.

Uncertain document tiers, partial payments, cross-currency amendments, ambiguous source identity and missing observation dates deliberately remain unresolved. Image metadata has no timestamp: the later evidence layer must establish observation context rather than silently assume it. No image values from real samples are fixtures in production.

## Request integration and completeness (Checkpoint C)

`forecast_with_evidence(profile, request, events, rates, sources, batches)` returns an EvidenceForecastResult with the existing ForecastResult, normalized state, decisions/amendments and source issues. `forecast_request(dataset, request, evidence_batches=...)` is the dataset entry point; omitted batches preserve legacy behavior exactly. Dataset sources are constructed from actual message/image metadata. Callers cannot replace these canonical associations with candidate-supplied identities.

The engine receives normalized immutable events and explicit scoped projection directives. It retains original recurrence evidence so a next-pay-only raise cannot accidentally retrain the payroll bridge. Rescheduled events keep their original occurrence identity for monthly suppression, preventing the old credit from reappearing or the following month from disappearing. Final-pay/termination directives suppress matched inferred continuation. Explicitly confirmed one-off future credits pass a typed confirmation set; pending credits are still excluded. FX and all balance/affordability arithmetic remain in the financial engine. Baseline policy constants are untouched.

Every source requires an exhaustive batch. A missing batch, partial interpretation, empty batch, rejected schema/context, unresolved fact, missing required amount, missing rate or ambiguous recurring target keeps the result provisional. Clearly hypothetical, conditional, nonfinancial and valuation facts can be rejected and resolved. A cancelled missing-amount obligation no longer requires its amount; the engine records cancellation rather than inventing zero cash. Provenance survives in normalized amendments, flow reasons and decision notes.

Synthetic fixtures cover the full pipeline. Real requests are not populated with hand-read facts. The metadata inventory and empty-adapter regression reproduce with `.venv\Scripts\python.exe code/evaluation/evidence_inventory.py`; full tests use `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m unittest discover -s tests -v`.

## Adversarial hardening (Checkpoint D)

`evidence_scope.bind_series_amendments` binds directives to supported original history before the forecast. Exact event/lifecycle identities and normalized descriptions identify a series; generic payroll fallback cannot bridge different named employers. An unsupported or ambiguous recurring target reverts the whole affected fact and returns UNRESOLVED/UNSUPPORTED_SCOPE. Overlapping different amounts for the same series, including a separately amended next-pay occurrence, revert the involved facts with CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE. Non-overlapping scopes remain independent. Cross-target precedence is intentionally conservative; no input ordering chooses a winner.

An accepted ongoing amount applies to both explicit future occurrences and inferred occurrences within its effective/end dates. It replaces each occurrence's amount and retains its identity and provenance. It never adds an extra occurrence or retrains historical recurrence. Termination stops supported inferred continuation; a separately confirmed final payment remains. Cancellation and reschedule scopes stay event/next-occurrence specific. Conflicting explicit cancellation and settlement need supported precedence or remain unresolved.

Transport rejects duplicate JSON fields and non-boolean exhaustive flags. An unlinked past-due promise without settlement is unresolved, not opening cash. Refund/sale/invoice semantics must match a linked event's financial type. Missing-amount exemptions require an actual accepted cancellation; an empty evidence adapter cannot silently improve baseline completeness. The review and regression evidence are in `evaluation/phase3a-review.md`.

## Phase 3B integration obligations

The extractor supplies candidates and coverage; it cannot supply a balance, authorize a rate, or promote its own publisher identity into trusted metadata. Source records must be constructed by the dataset adapter. `CONFIRMED` for new one-off cash means an unconditional, dated disbursement, not a pending estimate, approval contingent on further work, or an investment valuation. A pending CSV credit still requires SETTLED evidence; historical settlement is already represented by the opening snapshot.

Return UNRESOLVED for uncertain amount labels, dates, links, conditionality, or source coverage. Numeric fields in a provisional forecast are diagnostic only. Real image observation dates and publisher identity need an explicit trusted-context design in their later phase. Date-tiered bills, partial-paid arithmetic without an explicit balance, and complex overlapping amendments remain represented but unresolved. No inference from solved output columns is permitted.
